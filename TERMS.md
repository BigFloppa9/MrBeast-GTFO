## 2. What the bot is for

The bot detects one pattern: one account posting several images in several different channels within seconds (hijacked accounts spreading fake giveaway or cheat-download images). When the rule fires, it applies the punishment chosen by the server administrator (timeout or ban), deletes the account's recent messages and posts a report to a log channel.

You may use the bot only for moderation of servers you own or are authorized to moderate. You may not use it, or modify it, to monitor, profile or collect data about users, to harass users, or to act against users for any reason other than the configured rule.

## 4. Automated actions

Detection is automatic and can be wrong. A legitimate user who posts many images in many channels quickly may be timed out or banned. Server administrators should choose thresholds and punishments suitable for their community and review reports in the log channel. A punished user can be restored by the server's moderators with the normal Discord tools.
